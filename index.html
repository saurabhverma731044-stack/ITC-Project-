The 4 Core Types of Cakes & Their Features

Every cake in the world belongs to a fundamental structural family based on how air and fat are incorporated into the batter.

🧈

Butter / Shortened Cakes

Rich, velvety, dense & luxurious

Built upon the creamed-butter method where sugar and butter crystals aerate together before eggs and flour are folded in.

Structure: Tender crumb, tight grain
Leavening: Baking powder + soda
Examples: Pound cake, Red Velvet, Yellow cake
Best For: Carved multi-tier celebration cakes
☁️

Foam & Sponge Cakes

Feather-light, airy, bouncy & springy

Relies almost entirely on whipped whole eggs or separated whites (meringue) to trap air. Contains little to no fat.

Structure: Elastic, porous & moisture-absorbing
Leavening: Steam & whipped egg foam
Examples: Genoise, Angel Food, Swiss Rolls
Best For: Syrup-soaked rolls & fruit shortcakes
🌿

Chiffon Cakes (Hybrids)

Silky moisture meets supreme cloud fluffiness

The golden combination! Combines whipped egg whites for cloud-like height with pure vegetable oil for eternal refrigeration moisture.

Structure: Soft, springy, never stiffens when chilled
Leavening: Meringue fold + baking powder
Examples: Pandan Chiffon, Earl Grey, Citrus Chiffon
Best For: Delicate afternoon tea snacking
🍫

Custard, Mousse & Flourless

Melt-in-mouth, decadent, gooey or silky

Cakes where wheat flour takes a backseat or is omitted completely. Texture is achieved through baked custards or chilled Bavarian mousses.

Structure: Creamy, fudge-like, or set gelée
Leavening: Egg coagulation / none
Examples: Basque Cheesecake, Chocolate Torte, Tiramisù
Best For: Fine-dining desserts & gluten-sensitive guests

🔬 The Chemistry Behind Every Perfect Crumb

Understanding how moisture, structure, fat, and sugar create balance.

💧 Moisture Balance

Liquids (milk, buttermilk, fruit purées) dissolve sugars and hydrate proteins, activating gluten formation while ensuring a juicy mouthfeel.

🧈 Fat Coating

Butter and oil coat flour molecules to shorten gluten strands—which is why tender, soft-crumb cakes are traditionally called "shortened".

🫧 Leavening Kinetics

Heat causes baking soda or whipped air pockets to expand into carbon dioxide gas, lifting the batter before starches gelatinize to lock in the height.

✨ Sugar Tenderizing

Beyond sweetness, sugar is hygroscopic: it locks in moisture molecules, preventing the cake from drying out over several days.

Cake Care & Slicing Mastery

To experience each cake at peak flavor and texture, follow these patisserie care rituals.

🌡️

Serving Temperature

Butter cakes should be enjoyed at room temperature (leave out 45 mins before slicing). Mousse and chiffon shortcakes are best chilled straight from the fridge.

🔪

The Warm Knife Trick

Run a chef’s knife under hot tap water, wipe it completely dry with a clean cloth, and slice downwards. Wipe clean between every single slice for photo-ready edges!

📦

Airtight Preservation

Press parchment paper directly onto exposed cake crumb before covering in an airtight cake dome. Keeps fresh for 4 to 5 days without absorbing fridge odors.

❄️

Freezing Celebration Slices

Wrap individual leftover slices in cling film, then foil. They freeze gracefully for up to 2 months! Thaw overnight in your refrigerator.
